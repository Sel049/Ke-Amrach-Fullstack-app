## Slide 2: Project Overview
**Mission Statement**
*Empowering Ethiopian farmers to sell their produce directly to buyers while providing buyers with access to fresh, locally-sourced agricultural products.*

**Key Objectives:**
- Eliminate intermediaries in agricultural trade
- Ensure fair prices for farmers
- Provide fresh, local produce to buyers
- Support Ethiopian agricultural ecosystem
- Bridge the gap between rural farmers and urban consumers

---
